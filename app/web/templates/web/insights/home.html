Public-interest auditing

Every controlled prescription, on a record nobody can rewrite, and open to everyone.

Following every controlled prescription across every pharmacy is hard for any health department, and each gap in that control is a chance for a prescription filled past its limit, or a record that no longer matches what happened. RxTrail puts every prescription and every fill on Solana. The rules are enforced by a program no one controls, and the history is public data that anyone can audit.

Transparent for professionals, private for patients.

Limits no one can exceed

The sum dispensed never passes what was prescribed, even with two pharmacies racing for it. The program refuses, not a policy.

A history no one rewrites

Every act is signed by whoever did it and kept as its own account. Not even the operator, who pays the fees, can alter or erase a record.

Action in one second, everywhere

A recall, or a prescriber's leaked key suspended, reaches every pharmacy in the country at once.

Nothing about patients

The chain holds no name, document or pseudonym of any patient. What it shows is what professionals did.

The public record, right now

Read live from the program's accounts and the open catalog. Anyone can download the same data and get the same numbers: no trust in us required.
