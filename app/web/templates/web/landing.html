Controlled prescriptions, on Solana

Every pill accounted for.
Every record provable.

RxTrail puts the dispensing limit of controlled medication on a shared ledger no single party controls. A prescription can never be filled past what was prescribed — not even by two pharmacies at the same time — and anyone can audit its full history.

Try the demo Audit it yourself

The audit is open to anyone, no sign-in. Or read the source code.

Limits no one can bypass

The rule runs in an on-chain program. The total dispensed can never exceed the quantity prescribed.

Signed by who acted

Prescribers and pharmacies sign with their own keys. The operator pays the fees but cannot act for anyone.

Auditable, yet private

Only random ids and a document hash go on-chain. Patients' data stays off-chain.

Public-interest auditing

Don't trust us.
Audit it yourself.

Every prescription and every fill is a public account on Solana. The insights read them live, with the open catalog, and look for patterns worth a closer look: a drug pushed far above peers, a pharmacy that avoids generics, a brand locked again and again, a volume out of line.

No sign-in, no access to ask for, no trust in us: anyone can download the same data and get the same numbers. Nothing about patients is on the chain.
