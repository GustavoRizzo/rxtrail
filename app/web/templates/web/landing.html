Controlled prescriptions, on Solana

Every pill accounted for.
Every record provable.

RxTrail puts the dispensing limit of controlled medication on a shared ledger no single party controls. A prescription can never be filled past what was granted — not even by two pharmacies at the same time — and anyone can audit its full history.

Try the demo Source code

Limits no one can bypass

The rule runs in an on-chain program. The total dispensed can never exceed the quantity granted.

Signed by who acted

Prescribers and pharmacies sign with their own keys. The operator pays the fees but cannot act for anyone.

Auditable, yet private

Only random ids and a document hash go on-chain. Patients' data stays off-chain.
